About

I am a Research Assistant at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, where I also completed an MSc in Biomedical Engineering. My academic background spans environmental microbiology, functional biomaterials, and biomedical engineering.

My current research investigates magnetic bead-based bacterial capture and enrichment, with potential applications in rapid microbial diagnostics and antimicrobial susceptibility testing. I have also worked on antimicrobial gelatin–chitosan films, bacterial isolation and characterization, and nanomaterial-related research. I am particularly interested in applied bioengineering, microbial diagnostics, and functional biomaterials.

Research Experience

Research Assistant | Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

Sep 2026 – Present

Fc-MBL Magnetic Bead-Based Bacterial Capture

Investigating magnetic bead-based bacterial capture and enrichment for potential applications in rapid microbial diagnostics.

  • Evaluating Fc-MBL-functionalized magnetic beads for capture of Escherichia coli and Staphylococcus aureus at low bacterial concentrations.
  • Using magnetic separation, bacterial culture, OD600 measurements, and CFU-based assays to assess bacterial capture efficiency.
  • Comparing capture performance between bacterial species to identify experimental limitations and guide further optimization.

Master's Research Project | Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

2025 – 2026

Functional Gelatin–Chitosan Films for Food Preservation

Developed gelatin–chitosan composite films incorporating tannic acid–zinc modifications for functional food-packaging applications.

  • Fabricated composite films and investigated their surface wettability, water vapor barrier properties, antioxidant activity (DPPH/ABTS), and antibacterial performance.
  • Evaluated food-preservation performance using fresh-cut apple experiments, including weight loss and browning index measurements.
